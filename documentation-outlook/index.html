Documentation outlook

Can the past year tell us what the record will look like next? A little. Here is the plainest possible guess, and everything it leaves out.

Written from the Archive Genocide records · about three minutes to read

Read this first

This page guesses how many posts the archive will take in. That number depends on which channels people are saving, on platform bans and on news cycles as much as on events in Gaza. It predicts nothing about the war, the people in it, or when it ends.

1.If the last year simply continues

Lay a ruler along the past twelve months and extend the line. The archive would take in about — posts a month over the next six months, against about — a month this past year.[1]

The bands are wide on purpose. A straight line is the humblest model there is, and it should look humble.

2.Four words, six months on

The same ruler, laid along the share of posts that carry four words. Read the range, not the arrow.

3.What a ruler can't see

A ceasefire. A new channel saved, or an old one banned. A siege. Any one of them would bend these lines more than the trend ever could.

The record's future is not written in its past. It depends on what happens next, and on whether anyone is still there to film it.

Notes
  1. Method: a least-squares straight line through the last twelve complete months, carried six months forward. The range is the line plus or minus 1.96 times the spread of the past year's misses. The current month is left out because it is still in progress.
