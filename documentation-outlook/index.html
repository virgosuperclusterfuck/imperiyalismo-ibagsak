Documentation outlook

Can the past year tell us what the record will look like next? A little. Here is the plainest possible guess, and everything it leaves out.

!! READ FIRST !! This page forecasts archiving, not events. It guesses how many posts the archive will take in, a number shaped by which channels are being saved, platform bans and news cycles as much as by what happens on the ground. It predicts nothing about the war, its victims, or its end.

What a trend can't see

      
A ceasefire. A new channel saved, or an old one banned. A siege. Any one of them would bend these lines more than the trend ever could.

      
The record's future is not written by its past. It depends on what happens next, and on whether anyone is still there to film it.
