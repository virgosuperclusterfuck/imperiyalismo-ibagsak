Documentation outlook

A straight-line projection of archive intake over the next six months.

Source: Archive Genocide records API

Scope
  • Projects archiving volume only: records posted and saved.
  • Does not predict events, casualties, or the course of the war.
Summary
  • Projected intake: about — records a month over the next six months.
  • Past twelve months: about — records a month.
  • The uncertainty range is wide; see the shaded bands in Figure 1.

  • Ceasefires or escalations.

Method
  1. Least-squares line through the last twelve complete months, extended six months. Range: line ± 1.96 × the standard deviation of the past year's residuals. The current month is excluded.
