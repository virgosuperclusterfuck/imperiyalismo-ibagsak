Archive overview

What was kept

A summary of the Archive Genocide records: footage posted from Gaza since October 2023, saved before it could be deleted or lost.

Source: Archive Genocide records API

Summary
  • Records: — videos and photographs, each with its caption, posting time and source channel.
  • Sources: — separate collections, mostly Telegram channels.
  • Video: — of dated records.
  • Mapped: — of records carry a map location (— records).
  • Busiest month: —, with — records.
  • Withdrawn: — records removed since they were saved; excluded from every page.

1.Records per month

  • Each tower is one month; its height is the number of records saved from that month.
  • The busiest month is drawn in red.
  • Monthly volume reflects archiving activity as well as events (see Who keeps the record).

2.Most frequent caption words

  • Counts of records whose caption contains each word.
  • Arabic captions are matched through their English translation.
  • One record can contain several words.

3.Location data

  • — records have a map location.
  • Four precision levels: town named in the caption; neighbourhood or camp; named building; exact site geolocated from the footage.
  • Exact-site geolocation requires manual verification and covers only a small share of mapped records (see The numbers).

Limits
  • A record is a post, not a casualty count. Unfilmed or lost events are absent.
  • The archive holds only what was saved; its gaps reflect how it was collected.
  • Captions are the posters' own words: testimony, not verified findings.
