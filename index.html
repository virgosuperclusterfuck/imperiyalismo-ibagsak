Every post, kept

Since October 2023, people in Gaza have filmed what was happening around them and posted it — to Telegram, to TikTok, to anyone watching. Many of those posts were later deleted, banned or lost. Volunteers saved what they could. This is the archive they built, and the way in.

Records preserved

—

Each record is one video or photograph, the words its poster wrote beside it, the moment it went up, and the channel it came from. — of them are video.

A record is a testimony of what someone saw and chose to share. It is not, on its own, a verified finding — and the archive is careful to say so.

Every month, someone pressed record

Here is the archive as a skyline: one tower for each month, as tall as the number of posts saved from it. The tallest is —, when — posts went up in a single month — the season the north was besieged.

Where they stood

      
Only — records — — of the archive — can be placed on a map at all. Most of those can only be placed in a town or camp. Pinning a video to an exact building takes hours of careful work, matching skylines and street corners frame by frame.
