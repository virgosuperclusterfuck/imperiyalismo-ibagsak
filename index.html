Data: Archive Genocide (archivegenocide.com), a public read-only archive of posted footage from Gaza and the West Bank. Footage belongs to its filmers and captions to their authors; a record documents what was posted and when, not a finding. Review status is the archive's internal review state. Withdrawn records are excluded. Snapshot taken —; the archive itself updates continuously.
