Who keeps the record

An archive like this does not collect itself. Every record here was rescued, channel by channel, by people who noticed that posts were vanishing and decided to keep them.

Not one feed but many rescues

The archive is made of — separate collections: Telegram channels, TikTok accounts, a Discord server, each copied out and preserved on its own before it could disappear.

Telegram alone carries — of everything. It was where Gaza's journalists and local channels posted first, and fastest.

A city of rescues

Here every row is one collection, and every tower one month of it. The back row gathers all the smaller collections together. A row that suddenly stops is a channel that went quiet, was banned, or whose rescue covered a fixed window of time.

Why it matters

Today only — of the — collections are still growing.

So when the record thins, ask first: did events thin, or did a channel go dark? Every chart in this archive carries that question, and the honest answer is often both.
