Who keeps the record

An archive does not collect itself. Somebody noticed the posts were disappearing, and stayed up copying them.

Written from the Archive Genocide records · about four minutes to read

In short

The archive is not one feed. It is dozens of separate rescues of Telegram channels, TikTok accounts and a Discord server, each copied out before it could vanish. Most of those rescues have now stopped. That matters, because when a big one stops, the record thins whether or not anything changed on the ground.

  1. The archive is made of — separate collections.
  2. Telegram channels alone account for — of everything.
  3. Only — of them are still growing today.

1.Many rescues, not one feed

Gaza's journalists and local channels posted first, and fastest, on Telegram. Later, ordinary people posted on TikTok. When channels were banned or accounts fell silent, volunteers copied what was left. Each copy became a collection of its own, with its own beginning and its own end.

2.A city of rescues

In this figure every row is one collection and every tower is one month of it. The back row gathers the smaller collections together. A row that suddenly stops is a channel that went quiet, was banned, or whose rescue only covered a fixed stretch of time.

The largest single collection holds — of the whole archive. When its row ends, a large part of the record ends with it.

3.Why it matters

Of the — collections, most have stopped growing.[1] So before you read anything into a quiet month in the other papers, ask the plain question first: did less happen, or did a channel go dark?

The honest answer is often both.

What this can't tell you
  • Platforms are read from each collection's name, so a mixed collection counts under one platform.
  • Dates are posting dates, as preserved from each source. They are not always the date something happened.
  • Records withdrawn from the archive since they were saved appear nowhere in these pages.
Notes
  1. A collection counts as still growing if its newest post is within 60 days of the newest post anywhere in the archive.
